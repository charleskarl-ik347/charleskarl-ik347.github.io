Evidence Mapping of OPM-MEG and SQUID-MEG Readiness for Mild Traumatic Brain Injury — MIT IEEE URTC

As a co-first author, I conducted a comparative evidence-mapping study evaluating the clinical readiness of OPM-MEG and SQUID-MEG for mild traumatic brain injury (mTBI) detection. I reviewed and verified 39 scientific and technical sources, examining pathological slowing, alpha-band disruption, phase-amplitude coupling,and MEG system performance. Our study identified gaps between the technical capabilities of OPM-MEG and the clinical evidence required for its use in mTBI assessment.

Current Status: Presented at IEEE MIT URTC 2026. IEEE Xplore publication pending.

Gravitational Waves Simulation

A project from my AP Calculus BC Class to demonstrate Fourier Analysis on Grvaitaional Waves. In this case, I had generate a simple chirp signals, use numpy to perform a Fourier Transform, and then plot the results using Javascript. I later expanded the project to incorporate publicly available gravitational-wave data from the Gravitational Wave Open Science Center (GWOSC). This project was hosted by using render.

Computational Optics

Through R&D experience, I explored numerical modeling of optical and thermal systems, examining how mathematical assumptions, discretization, and boundary conditions influence simulation results. I later prepared a technical presentation introducing computational optics, including ray and wave optics, finite-difference time-domain methods, and numerical stability.
